08 / 09 Questions worth asking
Why is every figure a range?

Because the published measurements disagree by an order of magnitude, and a single number would be a claim we cannot support.

What happens when you do not know the model?

We print the word unknown. Rounding a missing value down to nought would make the tool lie in the flattering direction, which is the one direction it must never lie in.

Does any of this leave my machine?

No. There is no backend, no account and no telemetry.
